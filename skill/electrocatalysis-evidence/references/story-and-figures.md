# Argument, figure, phenomenon and title workflow

Resolve the clone via library-location.md. Read relevant sections of the2026-10-26 handbook, not the whole183-record library. E152–E183 have184 main figure groups, original authored observations/inference limits/bridges; eight SI audits are targeted rather than exhaustive. New32 include27 primary,4 reviews,1 commentary. Earlier151 scopes remain unchanged.

1. Define the unresolved question, controlled variable, competing explanations and decisive observable. Novel material identity is not automatically a new principle.
2. Retrieve examples AND contrary observations: `scripts/story_query.py --query "强度"`, `--card E160`, `--card E183`, or `--si SI05`. Use exact DOI/figure/physical page. Main E183 is local SI06, pages1–24; its SI is25–68.
3. For every panel record source type, sample/batch, conditions, axes/units/denominator, transforms, independent-repeat level, observation, supported inference, alternatives and next discriminating check.
4. Choose whole-paper architecture based on the scientific problem: benefit-first, working-state-first, orthogonal perturbation, anomaly, reversible loop, multiscale chain or review map. One main figure, one central claim; don't hide decisive negative controls in SI.
5. Speak/write condition→observation→bounded inference→next question. Do not equate Raman intensity with rate, ECSA normalization with intrinsic activity, state ΔG with a TS barrier, stable current with stable product or static model charge with regenerative electron turnover.
6. Pair title strength to evidence: measured outcome→controlled process→causal principle. Terms such as regenerable/buffering/spillover require separate evidence, not a DFT graphic alone. PyCEE–CuNi plans are conditional; do not edit manuscripts or invent measurements from examples.
7. Use Origin `.opju` and native PowerPoint/vector sources; ChemDraw/VESTA/Fiji/Blender as needed. Fixed sample colors plus symbols; sequential scales for ordered quantities; diverging scales only with a meaningful center. Software recommendations are ours, actual author disclosures require locators.
8. Audit with `scripts/audit_argument.py` and final human source/visual review. Automated flags are incomplete. Exact target-journal requirements must be refreshed at submission; distinguish current official specification, published example, and our preset.
